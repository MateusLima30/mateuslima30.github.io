Research

Understanding operations
as sociotechnical systems.

My research examines how technology, people, and operational processes interact, with particular attention to maintenance, safety, manufacturing, and warehousing environments.

01

Sociotechnical Dynamics

How technologies, workers, and organizational processes interact to shape operational outcomes.

02

Maintenance Operations

How maintenance practices, condition-based guidelines, equipment care, and technology investments affect operations.

03

Safety Management

How operational systems, workload, technologies, and managerial practices influence safety and risk.

04

Manufacturing & Warehousing

Empirical research in technology-rich manufacturing, intralogistics, and material-handling environments.
