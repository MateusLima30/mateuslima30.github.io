About me

I am an Assistant Professor of Operations and Supply Chain Management at the Kelley School of Business, Indiana University Indianapolis.

My research examines sociotechnical dynamics in operations, with a focus on maintenance and safety in manufacturing and warehousing. Much of my work draws on collaborations with industry partners and uses operational and Industrial IoT data to study how technology, people, and processes interact in operational settings.

My research has appeared in leading journals, such as the Journal of Operations Management and Decision Sciences, and has received numerous recognitions.
