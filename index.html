Mateus do Rego Ferreira Lima

Assistant Professor of Operations and Supply Chain Management

My research examines sociotechnical dynamics in operations, with a focus on maintenance and safety in manufacturing and warehousing. Much of my work draws on industry collaborations and leverages operational and Industrial IoT data to examine how technology, people, and processes interact in operational settings.

I received an M.S. in Industrial Engineering from the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (Brazil) and B.S. degrees in Industrial Engineering from the State University of Maranhão (Brazil) and the Milwaukee School of Engineering.
