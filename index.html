Assistant Professor of Operations and Supply Chain Management

I'm an Assistant Professor at the Kelley School of Business at Indiana University, Indianapolis. My research examines sociotechnical dynamics in operations, with a focus on maintenance and safety in manufacturing and warehousing. Much of my work draws on collaborations with industry partners and uses operational and Industrial IoT data to study how technology, people, and processes interact in operational settings.
